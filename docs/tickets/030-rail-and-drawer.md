# 030: A rail and a drawer

**Depends on:** 029

The app is too busy. The bottom tabs take a strip off every screen, every screen carries a "Tell it →" link in its header, and the task screen has a full-width button stuck to the bottom. Three ways into the same thing, on every screen.

Move the navigation to a rail down the left-hand side, and take the chat out of the navigation altogether. One round button in the bottom-right corner pulls the chat out of the right-hand side as a drawer, over whatever you were reading. The drawer knows what you were looking at, so on a task screen it opens with that task in focus, and the sticky "Tell it what you've done" bar goes away with it.

Nothing else changes: the same screens, the same information, less furniture around it.

**Done when:** the navigation is a rail on the left, the chat is a drawer pulled from the right by one button, and no screen carries a second way into the chat.
