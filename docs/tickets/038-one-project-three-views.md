# 038: One project, three views

**Depends on**: 037

The rail had three tabs for the same thing: Graph, Plan and What now. The map answers "what's left and what next", and the run of jobs answers "what can I do now" — the plan was a third answer to a question the first two already answered.

But the plan is not the same as the map. It shows the **nesting** — a group broken into its parts, and a part broken into its parts — all at once, and it reads as a list you can work down. The map shows the shape of the whole project at a glance, and its panel shows a group's parts one level at a time. So the plan is worth keeping as a way of looking; it is not worth a place of its own in the rail.

So:

- The plan leaves the rail. The rail is now the places you actually go: Graph, What now, Shopping, Docs, Log, More.
- The three views of the project sit together in the switch that already paired Graph with What now: **Graph | Plan | What now**, on all three screens.
- The switch is one component now, so the three screens cannot drift apart.
- The More screen's link is now "The plan as a tree", since that is what it is.

**Done when**: the plan is still one tap away from either of the other two views, and the rail has one less thing in it.
