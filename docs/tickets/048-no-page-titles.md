# 048: No page titles

**Depends on**: 047

Every page opened with its own name — Shopping, Docs, Log, and What now with a sentence counting the things you could do — directly beside a rail that already says where you are. It was noise, and it made the pages look busier than the map.

Now Shopping, Docs, Log and What now open straight onto their content, with the same space above it. The sentence under What now went with its title.

A note and a drawing keep their titles: that is the name of the thing, not of the page.

**Done when**: no page repeats the name the rail already gives it.
