# 045: A label on the picked job

**Depends on**: 044

The next job of every group wore a label all the time. Together they crowded the edge of the map, and they said nothing about the job you were actually looking at.

Now:

- The next job of each group still glows, but no label is drawn until you pick a job.
- The job you pick gets the label, out towards the edge of the map and clear of the work.
- The label is a link: tapping it opens the task.

**Done when**: an unpicked map has no labels, a picked job has one, and tapping it opens the task.
