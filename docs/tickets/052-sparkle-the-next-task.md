# 052: Sparkle the next task

**Depends on**: 051

Each group has a job it would do next, and on the map it only glowed a little, while What now didn't say which jobs those were at all.

Now the next job of each group stands out on both:

- **On the map**, its glow breathes and two small stars twinkle beside it.
- **In What now**, the card in Ready now has a shimmer that sweeps across it and a star in the corner that twinkles. Only the jobs that are a group's next get it, so the recommended work stands out from the rest of the column.
- With reduced motion turned on, nothing moves: the glow and the stars stay still.

**Done when**: the next job of each group is easy to pick out on the map and in What now.
