# 044: A progress ring round each group

**Depends on**: 043

The group dots on the map said which group a job belonged to, but not how far along the group was; you had to look at the rail for that.

Now each group's dot has a ring round it that fills in green, clockwise from the top, by the share of the group that is done. The percentage is the group's own roll-up, so it is the same number the rail shows.

**Done when**: each group on the map shows its progress as a ring, and it matches the rail.
