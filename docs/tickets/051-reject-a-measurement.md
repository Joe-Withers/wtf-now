# 051: Reject a measurement

**Depends on**: 050

A note the AI has written that holds a measurement asks "Is this right? Check it before cutting anything", and offered one answer: "Yes, it's right". If it wasn't, there was nothing to tap, so the owner had to describe the mistake in the chat, and the AI wrote a second note beside the wrong one (fixed in 050).

Now there's a second answer, "No, it's wrong". It removes the note, shows an undo, and takes the owner back to the docs. It says what to do next: tell the chat the right figures, and it writes them down again.

This is the fourth owner tap, the other half of confirming a measurement: it is the owner's judgement, not a record of work, so it belongs on the screen rather than in the chat.

**Done when**: an unverified measurement can be rejected with a tap and the rejection undone.
